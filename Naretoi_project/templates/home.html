About Naretoi

Located in the heart of Maasailand, Kenya, Naretoi Charity Organisation is dedicated to sustainable community development.

Our Mission

To uplift communities in Maasailand through education, health, livelihood support, and ensuring access to clean water resources.

Our Vision

A thriving Maasailand community where every individual has access to quality education, healthcare, economic opportunities, and clean water.

Our Values

Compassion, integrity, accountability, and community-driven solutions guide everything we do.
